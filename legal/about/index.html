About GameVault

A browser games library that loads fast on a school laptop and gets out of the way.

What it is

GameVault is a static website. There is no server doing work behind it — the pages, the catalog and the games are plain files, which is why it loads on hardware that struggles with everything else.

The library is a couple of thousand titles. Almost all of them are stored on this site's own address, spread over a few sister sites so each stays quick to update. A few very large ones still load from where they came from. Either way you click a tile and it plays.

What it does beyond the games

  • A movie and TV section, for the days nobody wants to play anything.
  • An optional account that remembers your playtime, level and starred games across devices.
  • Chat, a math practice page, cosmetic crates earned by playing, and a weekly PolyTrack challenge.
  • Save backups: download your game progress, or keep it in the cloud.
  • A bell-schedule strip in the header, so you know how long you actually have.
  • Tab disguises and a panic key, under Settings.

Who runs it

One person, as a side project. Nothing here is sold, and there are no ads.

Credits

  • Site: built and run by the GameVault owner.
  • Games: made by their original developers. Many copies here come from the Strongdog collection (gametreexp.github.io), credited as its README asks, and from community collections such as gn-math.
  • Built with: Ruffle for Flash games, EmulatorJS and webretro for consoles, and Supabase for accounts.

Requests

Missing game, broken game, or an idea for a whole new section — the suggestion form is the fastest way in. Everything that comes through it ends up on the requests board.
