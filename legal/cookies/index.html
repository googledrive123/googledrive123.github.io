Cookies and local storage

GameVault sets no advertising cookies, because there are no ads.

What this site actually uses

GameVault does not set tracking cookies of its own. It uses your browser's local storage and session storage, which are similar but stay on your device and are never attached to outgoing requests.

Signed-in accounts

Signing in stores a Supabase session token in your browser so you stay signed in. Signing out removes it.

Other people's cookies

Games loaded from other hosts, the movie player, and Google Fonts may set their own storage or cookies inside their own frames. That is outside this site's control. Pages under /games/ also load Google Analytics, which sets its own cookies.

Clearing it

Clearing site data for this domain in your browser settings removes everything above. You will lose your starred games, your recents and your settings — nothing else.
