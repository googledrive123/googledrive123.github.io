Reporting a broken game

It helps a lot if you include the game's name and what happened — black screen, stuck loading, sound but no picture. Games that are loaded from another host break without warning and there is no alert when they do, so a report is genuinely the only way it gets noticed.

Copyright and takedowns

If you hold the rights to something hosted here and want it removed, send the game's name and the page it appears on through the form. It comes down. See the Terms for the longer version.
