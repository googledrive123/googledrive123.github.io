Privacy Policy

Written against what the code actually does, not a template.

1. Saved in your browser only

Most of what GameVault remembers never leaves your device. It lives in your browser's local storage under keys beginning with gv. and is readable only by this site:

  • gv.starred.v1 — the games you have starred.
  • gv.recent.v1 — the last handful of games you opened.
  • gv.pinnedcats.v1 — categories you pinned to the front of the bar.
  • gv.cloak.v1 — the tab disguise you picked, if any.
  • gv.sessions — play sessions recorded while signed out.
  • gv.sugg.v1 — whether you have dismissed the suggestion popup.
  • panicKey, panicRedirectUrl, verificationEnabled — your panic key and close-confirmation settings.

Clearing your browser data clears all of it. There is no copy kept anywhere else unless you sign in.

2. Accounts

Signing in is optional. The whole site works without one.

If you do create one, you pick a username and a password. No email address is asked for or stored — the account is registered internally as yourname@gamevault.app, an address that does not exist and cannot receive mail. Accounts are held by Supabase, which stores the username and a hashed password.

While signed in, each game you open is recorded as a play session — the game, when you started, when you stopped. That is what the playtime numbers on your profile are built from.

3. Site analytics

GameVault counts usage so the owner can tell which games are worth keeping. This runs first-party, in js/analytics.js, writing to the same Supabase project. It records:

  • A random visitor and session identifier. Not your name, not your IP address.
  • Page views, route changes and which games, movies or shows were opened.
  • Searches typed into the site, including ones that returned nothing.
  • Browser, operating system, device type, screen and window size, language, time zone.
  • Page load timing, and JavaScript errors, so breakage gets noticed.

Pages under /games/ additionally load Google Analytics (G-KRH3X9QS3M), which is subject to Google's privacy policy.

4. Turning analytics off

Open your browser console on this site and run:

localStorage.setItem('gv.noanalytics', '1')

The first-party tracker then stops sending anything, permanently, on this browser. Nothing else about the site changes.

5. Third parties

GameVault does not sell or share data. It does load things from other people's servers, and those servers see the request:

  • Games served from another host, shown in a frame.
  • Thumbnail images from public image CDNs.
  • Fonts from Google Fonts.
  • Movie and TV metadata from TMDB, with playback embedded from a third-party player.
  • Supabase, for accounts and analytics.

What those services do with a request is governed by their own policies, not this one.

6. Under 13

GameVault is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly hold their data. If an account needs removing, ask through the contact form.

7. Deleting your data

Clear your browser storage for everything local. For an account and its play history, ask through the contact form and it gets deleted.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the date below changes with it.
