Daily auto-save
Auto-save this browser
Once a day, when you open GameVault signed in, this browser's progress is copied to your auto-save slot, replacing the one before. Off until you turn it on, and only in browsers where you turned it on.

What a save holds

Every game that runs on GameVault itself keeps its progress in this browser, and a save holds all of it.

Some games still load from other sites, genizymath.github.io and icons-6.us, and keep their progress there. GameVault cannot see or back up those saves. They are being moved over, and each one is covered as soon as it plays from here.

PolyTrack is left out because it already syncs with your account. Your sign-in and tab disguise are left out too: they belong to this browser, not to a game.
