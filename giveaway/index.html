Giveaway

Enter with your GameVault account for a chance at a prize on the site: a badge, a crate or a name color. It is free, it is one entry each, and it is never money.

How it works
  • Free, one entry per account. Pressing Enter again does not add a second.
  • Only your username is used. You need a GameVault account to enter, and GameVault never asks for your email, real name, address or phone number.
  • The winner is picked at random by the server when the clock runs out, and their username is shown on this page.
  • Prizes are site things: a badge, a crate, a name color. Never money, gift cards, game currency, or anything sent to your home.
  • The site owner adds the prize to the winning account. Nobody from GameVault will message you asking for details, a password or a payment to claim it. If someone does, it is not us.
