    Rules
    • The prize is a badge. First place gets the Challenge winner badge, second and third get the Top 3 badge. Never money, gift cards, game currency, or anything sent to your home.
    • Times come from the PolyTrack leaderboard. Race the month's track in PolyTrack here on GameVault, and your best time set during the month counts. Times from before the month started do not.
    • Suspicious times get removed. A time that looks impossible, or came from a cheat or a glitch, is taken off and cannot win.
    • The month ends at midnight UTC. A tie goes to whoever set the time first.
    • Only your leaderboard name is shown. Guests can race too; sign in to keep your badge with your account. Anonymous mode keeps your name off this page as well.
